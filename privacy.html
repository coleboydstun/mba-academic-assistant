Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Purpose

MBA Academic Assistant is a personal academic organization application used by its developer.

Information Accessed

The application accesses Google Calendar event information with read-only authorization, including event titles, dates, and descriptions.

Information Storage

Calendar information is organized and stored in a Google spreadsheet controlled by the application developer.

Use of Information

Information is used for personal academic planning, assignment tracking, and calendar organization.

Data Sharing

The application does not currently send calendar event content to third-party AI services or sell personal information. Data is processed through Google Apps Script and Google services.

Security

The application uses Google OAuth authorization. Calendar access is read-only, and spreadsheet modification permissions are restricted to the spreadsheet containing the script.

Data Retention and Deletion

The developer controls the spreadsheet containing imported event information and can delete that information. Google account permissions can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application developer through the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
